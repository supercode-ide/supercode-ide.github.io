05 QUESTIONS & ANSWERS

Frequently Asked Questions

Troubleshooting `scanf` prompts, `fgets` newline quirks, `EOF`, and `fopen` paths on Android.

Why does `printf("Enter number: ");` show up immediately in SuperCode without `fflush(stdout)`?+
Standard C streams are often block-buffered when piped, hiding prompts that don't end with `\n`. SuperCode configures unbuffered standard streams automatically so interactive prompts appear before `scanf`, `fgets`, or `getchar` waits.
How do I send `Ctrl+D` (`EOF`) on an Android touchscreen keyboard?+
Whenever the console input bar is active and empty, tapping the Send button transmits `EOF` (`[EOF]`) cleanly to your running C process—ending `while (scanf(...) != EOF)` and `getchar() != EOF` loops.
How do I pass command-line arguments with spaces to `char *argv[]`?+
Write `// @args "My Report.txt" -n 10` near the top of your `.c` file. SuperCode's argument tokenizer preserves quoted strings as a single `argv[i]` entry and supports up to 32 arguments.
Where are files created with `fopen("output.txt", "w")` saved?+
They are synchronized directly into the same Android project folder as your `.c` source file, so they persist permanently and can be opened right inside SuperCode's editor.
